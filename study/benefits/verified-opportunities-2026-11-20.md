## Reminders, evidence and ethics

- Calendar advance alerts should be at least 4 days before every **verified** cut-off. A calendar reminder is not a submission or an eligibility decision.
- Many offers close 31 Dec. Prioritize cash-value voucher, student AI trial, then most relevant Excel/Power BI courses.
- Do not use personal contacts, IDs, banking credentials or hardship details in this public repo. No email, DM, or applications are sent by this register.
- Staff/individual account eligibility is authoritative; listing availability does not prove local store stock, account credit, or award.
- Check for paid auto-renewal before starting software trials and record cancellation date privately if claimed.
