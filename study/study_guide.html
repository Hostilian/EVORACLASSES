Use the cards and videos together

A suggested 70-minute session: 10 minutes recall cards → 15 minutes one matched explanation → 35 minutes independent practice, with two five-minute breaks. Divide it into a 25-minute and a 35-minute focus block. Video time is inside the session.

The browser helper’s “Needs practice” filter is useful for recall but does not implement Anki’s due-date scheduler. The existing 604 cards are preserved; no new import or completed deck is assumed.
