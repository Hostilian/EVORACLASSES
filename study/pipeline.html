How the daily plan is updated

  1. Capture sources. Keep originals unchanged with source links, capture dates, hashes, page counts and an explicit missing-items list. Read-only checks do not start quizzes or save official forms.
  2. Review requirements. Preserve official deadlines and uncertainty. Public suggestions exclude private mail, grades, identifiers, timetable and progress history.
  3. Plan within capacity. A seven-hour base and eight-hour weekly maximum include Anki, explanations, practice and breaks. Dated priorities replace existing blocks; they do not add catch-up hours.
  4. Report actual work. Use a real tick or Not done yet on the website. Untouched boxes remain unreported. Browser saves work without a private connection; cloud saves require its verified read-and-write confirmation.
  5. Review the next day. At most one explicitly unfinished priority can replace the next practice block. Missing phone data stays unknown. Changed tasks preserve earlier reports separately and receive no inherited completion tick.
  6. Deliver reminders after verification. A reminder preview and current-key delivery check come before scheduled messages. Duplicate protection preserves delivery state privately; uncertain delivery is not automatically retried.

Official submissions, registrations, grades, changed instructions and future resources remain authoritative in Moodle or the official student portal. Website reports do not alter those systems, Google Calendar or native Anki scheduling.
